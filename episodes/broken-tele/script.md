# BROKEN TELE

**Boy Parent Problems.** A short family comedy, about 90 seconds. Strong language.

**The story:** A boy of very few words breaks the telly. Dad buys a new one. Toys are banned, then allowed back,
soft ones only. The boy breaks the new telly with a soft ball. His parents give up on tellies, and the boy looks at
the projector.

**Cast:** The Boy (`family-boy`, the real recording: he only ever says "TV's broken"), Mum (`family-mum`),
Dad (`family-dad`), and the Shop Assistant (off screen). Mum, Dad and the Shop Assistant are stand-in
text-to-speech voices until real recordings arrive.

**Where everyone is:** the living room has the sofa on the left of the frame, the telly on its unit on the right.
Mum sits in the armchair, screen left, facing right towards the telly. The boy plays on the rug between them, so
Mum looks right to him and the telly; the boy looks left to Mum and right to the telly.

## SCENE 1: FIVE MINUTES OF PEACE

**INT. LIVING ROOM. DAY.**

*Mum sinks into the armchair with her phone. The boy plays on the rug with his toy car, the dinosaur and blocks.*

[L001] MUM: Five minutes of peace. That's all I want.

*Vroom. The car goes up the dinosaur's back. It drifts towards the telly.*

[L002] MUM: Oi. Not near the telly. You'll break something.

*The boy looks at Mum. He looks at the car. He looks at the telly. He gives Mum his best smile. She narrows her
eyes, then goes back to her phone. He gets up and winds up the car.*

[L003] MUM: Don't you dare.

*SMASH. The car goes through the screen. Silence. A crack spreads. One shard drops and tinkles. Mum's face. The
boy turns to the telly, then to us, and points.*

[L004] BOY: TV's broken.

[L005] MUM: Yes. Thank you. Mummy can see that.

## SCENE 2: THE CALL

**INT. DAD'S OFFICE. DAY.** *Dad's phone buzzes on the desk. Cut between them.*

[L006] DAD: Hiya love.
[L007] MUM: Your son's broken the telly.
[L008] DAD: Is he alright?
[L009] MUM: He's fine. He's stood next to it. Pointing at it.

*Cut to the boy, still pointing at the telly, perfectly content.*

[L010] DAD: For fuck's sake. I've only just paid it off.

## SCENE 3: THE EXPENSIVE WAY HOME

**INT. DAD'S CAR. DAY.**

[L011] DAD: Could've thrown a cushion. Could've thrown a sock. No. A car. A metal car.

**INT. ELECTRONICS SHOP. DAY.** *Dad trudges down the aisle of tellies.*

[L012] SHOP: Same one again, mate?
[L013] DAD: Same one again.

*Insert: the card reader. £799. A beep. APPROVED.*

**INT. HALLWAY / LIVING ROOM. DAY.** *Dad staggers in with the big box. He sets up the new telly; it gleams.*

[L014] DAD: Right. This one had better see Christmas.

*Mum marches past with the box of toys.*

[L015] MUM: And toys are banned. All of them.

*The boy, arms crossed, sulks.*

## SCENE 4: TWO WEEKS LATER

*Title card: TWO WEEKS LATER.*

[L016] DAD: He's learned his lesson. Toys can come back in. Soft ones.
[L017] MUM: On your head be it.

*Title card: TWO MINUTES LATER.*

*The boy sneaks in with a soft red ball behind his back, finger to his lips: to us. He winds up. SMASH.*

*Mum and Dad run in.*

[L018] DAD: What the f...

*The boy points.*

[L019] BOY: TV's broken.

[L020] MUM: Yes. We can see that.

## SCENE 5: THE SOLUTION

*Mum and Dad slumped on the sofa in front of the broken telly.*

[L021] DAD: How? It's a soft ball.
[L022] MUM: I can't do this anymore. We're getting a projector.
[L023] DAD: Yeah. Probably for the best.

*Days later. A projector on the ceiling throws a big bright picture of a dinosaur on the wall. The boy, on the
rug, slowly looks up at the projector. Then at the ball in his hand.*

[L024] DAD: Don't. You. Dare.

*The boy smiles at us. Hard cut to black. SMASH. Title: BROKEN TELE.*

*After the title, in the dark:*

[L025] BOY: TV's broken?
