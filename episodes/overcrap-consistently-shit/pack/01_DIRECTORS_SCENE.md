# Consistently Shit — Director's script

**Cast:** Mark Goldbridge and Gary Neville. Fictional, satirical football comedy. 35–40 seconds target, time driven by the recorded dialogue. Studio: reuse the existing Overcrap/Passmic set and seated blocking. Finish: understated rant vs exhausted deadpan.

## Beat 1 — The worst half (Mark)
**Camera**: `M` medium, cut to `Mc` close-up on **WORST**. **Mark**: angry-rant expression; arms down then both-hands-out on “since I was a bloody kid”, ending in a defeated slump.

MARK [furious]: “That is the WORST half of football I've seen since I was a bloody kid!”

## Beat 2 — Three weeks ago (Gary)
**Camera**: cut to `G` medium; very gentle push to `Gc` close-up for the punchline. **Gary**: tired/deadpan; arms folded or arms down. Look to Mark before the next line.

GARY [exhausted]: “I said three weeks ago that was the worst Manchester United I'd ever seen.”
GARY [dry, deadpan]: “Turns out they took it as a fucking challenge.”

Hold a short reaction with no mouth movement before cutting away.

## Beat 3 — The shovel (Mark)
**Camera**: two-shot (`2`) then medium Mark (`M`). **Mark**: leans in, points once then spreads his hands. Do not paste a floating shovel; the line is funny without one.

MARK [sarcastic]: “Honestly, Gary, every time we hit rock bottom, these idiots turn up with a bloody shovel!”

A small, natural beat. Mark slightly softens and shrugs.

MARK [attempting optimism]: “Still... at least we're consistent.”

## Beat 4 — Defeated agreement (Gary)
**Camera**: `Gc` sustained close-up. Gary looks at Mark, then turns to lens. Avoid an exaggerated grin or eye dart.

GARY [flat]: “Yeah.”

**Pause**: about half a second, adjusted to voice performance.

GARY [absolutely emotionless]: “We are consistent at being shit.”

Cut to Mark's silent nod and back to Gary if the reaction adds a laugh. **Final graphic:** “MANCHESTER UNITED — NEW LOW UNLOCKED” can be composited into an existing studio display, not baked into a new background. Hard cut to black.

## Sound and cut direction
No unnecessary music or whooshes. Dialogue is foremost; short, human pauses and reaction cuts are the joke. The closing line should be the least performed of all. Lips must close as each line finishes, including on reaction shots.
