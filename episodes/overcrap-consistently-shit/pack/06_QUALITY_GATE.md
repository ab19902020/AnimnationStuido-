# Animation quality gate — fail the render if any item is wrong

- [ ] Match source `Passmic` Mark and Gary character appearance exactly: no alternate face model, hair, body ratio or wardrobe.
- [ ] Use existing real character PNGs and rig anchors; no flattened character contact sheet as a sprite.
- [ ] No double eyes, floating/detached hands, cut shoulders, see-through neck, or off-position mouth patch.
- [ ] Close-mouth rest during silence and on listener reaction shots.
- [ ] Check consonant closure (M/B/P), F/V contact, stressed vowels, and one-frame lead into phonemes.
- [ ] M and Mc shots use Mark's corresponding original upper body and expression; same for G/Gc.
- [ ] The studio table occludes lower torsos correctly, and both eyelines agree between shots.
- [ ] Mark's gestures move on line stresses without random frantic motion; Gary barely moves.
- [ ] The final deadpan beat is given enough time to read.
- [ ] Inspect consecutive frames around gesture swaps to catch popping, slipping, or texture discontinuities.
- [ ] Make contact-sheet checks before encoding full video; 720p approval preview first, 4K only after approval.
- [ ] Keep sources/engine in repo and commit on a review branch, do not overwrite user-approved animation without comparison.
