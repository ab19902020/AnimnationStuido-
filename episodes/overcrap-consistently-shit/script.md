# OVERCRAP DAILY — CONSISTENTLY SHIT

**A fictional, satirical football comedy short** (portrait, for YouTube Shorts). Mark Goldbridge and Gary Neville at the
Overcrap Daily desk after another dreadful Manchester United half. The director's scene, cue map and quality gate are in
`pack/` (`01_DIRECTORS_SCENE.md`). Understated rant against exhausted deadpan; the reactions are the joke.

**INT. OVERCRAP DAILY STUDIO. NIGHT.** *Mark at the desk in his United hoodie, Gary in the chair beside him. Manchester
at night through the window behind them.*

[L001] MARK: That is the WORST half of football I've seen since I was a bloody kid!

*Furious. Crash in on WORST; a defeated slump at the end.*

[L002] GARY: I said three weeks ago that was the worst Manchester United I'd ever seen.

*Exhausted. He looks to Mark.*

[L003] GARY: Turns out they took it as a fucking challenge.

*Dry, deadpan. A short hold with no mouth movement.*

[L004] MARK: Honestly, Gary, every time we hit rock bottom, these idiots turn up with a bloody shovel!

*Sarcastic, increasingly frustrated; leans in, points once, then spreads his hands.*

[L005] MARK: Still... at least we're consistent.

*Attempting optimism; softens and shrugs.*

[L006] GARY: Yeah.

*Flat. About half a second.*

[L007] GARY: We are consistent at being shit.

*Absolutely emotionless, to the lens. Mark's silent nod; NEW LOW UNLOCKED; hard cut to black.*
