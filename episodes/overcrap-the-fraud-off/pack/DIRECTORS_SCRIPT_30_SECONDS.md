# DIRECTOR'S PRODUCTION SCRIPT
## OVERCRAP DAILY — “THE FRAUD OFF!”

**Format:** 30-second fictional animated parody of a football panel discussion  
**Cast:** Mark Goldbridge and Jamie Carragher, no extras required  
**Location:** the EXISTING Overlap / Overcrap-style studio from the animation project  
**Tone:** real pundit squabble turning into an absurd schoolyard shouting match  
**Aspect:** stage and animate using existing project's native framing; provide 9:16 social edit if the studio can reframe cleanly  
**Delivery:** 720p preview for approval; 4K final only after approval

### Director's vision
This is *not* a stand-up comedy routine, slideshow or a fight. It must feel like we have caught two people in an actual studio, mid-show, taking the piss out of each other. Think natural, clipped British football-chat rhythm: Jamie is smug and winds Mark up; Mark responds instantly and more personally; Jamie uses “Brent” like a trump card; Mark retaliates with the payslip accusation. End with both men looking ridiculous.

**The comedy arc:**
- Jamie thinks he owns the room at the opening and enjoys watching Mark react.
- Mark stays controlled just long enough to land the Everton/Liverpool dig.
- Jamie looks genuinely proud on “Champions League”, only to regret giving Mark the setup.
- “Brent” gets the biggest reaction pause *before* the final argument.
- Mark's “payslip” line is the longest and sharpest, filmed as an escalating rant.
- Jamie barks “FRAUD!”, Mark instantly fires back “BLUE NOSE!”, then a tiny freeze/abrupt cut.

### Acting notes and visual comedy
**Jamie:** breezy, cocky, enjoying himself. He should feel like he's *trying* to make Mark crack up. Smile between lines, glance to Mark and back to camera, tiny shoulder shrugs, smug side-eye. His confidence spikes on “Champions League” and again on “Brent”. “FRAUD” is absurdly loud but still playful.

**Mark:** quick-witted, initially controlled but visibly irritated. Begin with a raised eyebrow and a scoff, not instant fury. Use skeptical blinking, palms up, head shake, then escalating hand chopping / pointing in the long rant. Finish with a comically overcommitted “BLUE NOSE!” immediately after Jamie's shout. Mark should briefly believe he has won the argument.

**What makes it funny:** contrast and reactions, not random motion. Shoot the character who has just been insulted for a fraction of a second; allow expressions to register. Every cut should follow an insult, interruption, or punchline. Do not place huge captions over either face.

### Character art / rigging rules
- Preserve the existing show style: oversized heads, wide expressive white eyes, thick dark outlines, flat/cel shading and slightly exaggerated facial proportions. Studio clothing (not United jerseys). Keep costumes consistent between sheets, cuts and base models.
- Assets in `Character_Sheets/` are **four-pose transparent cutout sheets**, three per character; `Individual_Poses/` contains all 24 isolated PNGs. Use `POSE_MANIFEST.csv` for filenames and action labels.
- Separate head/eyes/eyebrows/mouth into the existing cutout rig if possible. Use the existing per-character AEIOU/phoneme mouth library. Mouths must be driven by the spoken words, **not** simply randomly cycled. Never show two faces, misplaced eyes, copied heads or detached hands.
- Idle motion: shallow torso sway, head nods, plausible blinks, breathing. Gesture on important words only. Hands/forearms do not intersect the table, microphone or the other person.
- Characters MUST be physically seated and grounded within the already-built studio. No hovering, sliding shoes, changing head size, abrupt pose snapping or sudden outfit changes.

### Audio and pacing
- Generate voice performances separately for each actor. Keep lines clean and dry; subtle studio room tone is fine.
- Use expression cues as direction, **not** spoken words. If using cloned/synthetic voices, work only with voices the studio is permitted to use and keep this framed as fictional satire/parody rather than a genuine clip.
- Word-align with the existing project lip-sync workflow/Whisper as appropriate, then hand-correct the emphasized words (“fraud”, “Everton”, “Liverpool”, “Brent”, “payslip”, “BLUE NOSE”).
- Music: optional low-volume sly/comedic tension bed; duck at least 15–20 dB under clear spoken dialogue. One short comic sting after the final exchange. Avoid repetitive meme boings, canned laughs or background music that competes with speech.
- The biggest *silence* is a split-second after Jamie says “Brent!” Don't waste seconds on establishing shots. Fast British speech is fine; garbled speech is not.

### Deliverables and acceptance checks
**Preview:** clear 720p MP4 with all lines, lip sync, transitions, captions optional, and all graphics in place. **Final after approval:** maximum-quality 4K render as supported by existing pipeline, plus an editable/reproducible production bundle and ideally a social-friendly crop. Do not upscale badly or fake extra animation with simple zooms.

**PASS only if:**
1. Exactly the approved words are used and each mouth moves only for its own voice.
2. Visual identities, outfits, body proportions and art style match the existing show from first to last shot.
3. Seated characters remain convincingly anchored with natural arm/table occlusion.
4. We get the facial reactions on *Forest*, *Everton*, *Liverpool*, *Brent* and *payslip*.
5. The *Brent* setup lands clearly and the final FRAUD/BLUE NOSE exchange is near-instantaneous, funny and readable.
6. The runtime lands close to 30 seconds with no clipped syllables, no rushed/slurred playback and no dead intro.
7. Frame edges/crops work on both wide set background and the selected social format.

**Producer directive:** Reuse established artwork and existing animation engine. Use the provided sprite sheets only to increase acting range. This package is a *new short skit*, not permission to rebuild previous episodes or replace established character models.
