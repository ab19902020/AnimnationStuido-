# OVERCRAP DAILY — THE FRAUD OFF!

**A 30-second fictional animated parody** of a football panel discussion. Mark Goldbridge and Jamie Carragher, two
pundits in a two-chair studio, mid-show, taking the piss out of each other until it turns into a schoolyard shouting
match. The dialogue is invented comedy, not a transcript. The director's script, shot list and acting notes are in
`pack/DIRECTORS_SCRIPT_30_SECONDS.md`.

**The lines as recorded** (`voiceovers/`). Where an actor's take differs from the pack's 30-second edit, the
recording wins and the pack's wording is noted below the line.

**INT. OVERCRAP DAILY STUDIO. NIGHT.** *Two armchairs angled in, a little round table, a stadium on the big screen.
Jamie (screen left) is already grinning. Mark (screen right) glances sideways, suspicious.*

[L001] JAMIE: First show and we've already got the biggest fraud in football! You're a Forest fan, Mark!

*Smug, laughing, teasing.*

[L002] MARK: That's rich coming from an Everton fan who spent his whole life pretending to love Liverpool!

*Dry, sarcastic, quick comeback. (Pack: "That's rich from an Everton fan who's spent his life pretending...")*

[L003] JAMIE: I won the Champions League with Liverpool, mate!

*Defensive pride, almost offended.*

[L004] MARK: Exactly! You've done more for Liverpool than Everton have in thirty years!

*Mocking, pleased with himself. A private laugh; Jamie's grin vanishes.*

[L005] JAMIE: At least I use me real name, Brent!

*Deadpan setup, then a delighted grin. The biggest silence of the piece comes after "Brent!".*

[L006] MARK: Oh, here we go. You supported Everton, played for Liverpool and now work for a Man United legend! You'll support anyone who gives you a payslip!

*Starts irritated, grows into a fast rant; finger jab on "payslip". (Pack: "Everton fan, Liverpool player, now working
for a United legend! You'll support anyone with a payslip!")*

[L007] JAMIE: FRAUD!

*Loud, laughing accusation.*

[L008] MARK: BLUE NOSE!

*Immediate shout, triumphant. A tiny freeze, both glaring; hard cut to black on the sting.*
