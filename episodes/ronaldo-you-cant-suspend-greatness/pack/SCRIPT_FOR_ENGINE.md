# RONALDO — YOU CAN'T SUSPEND GREATNESS

**Fictional football cartoon parody — not a claim about a real suspension.** RONALDO = Cristiano Ronaldo. BRUNO = Bruno Fernandes. Build using the existing Animation Studio film engine and house-style characters. Target about 85–105 seconds depending on performances; 16:9, 30fps.

## SCENE 1 — THE BENCH HAS STARTED IT

INT. PORTUGAL DRESSING ROOM — DAY.

[Start mid-argument. Bruno holds a phone showing a fictional suspension notice. Ronaldo is polishing his own framed portrait. The bench is visible. No long intro.]

[L001] BRUNO: Cristiano. Portugal have suspended you.
[L002] RONALDO: Suspended? I'm not a bloody ceiling fan.
[L003] BRUNO: It's only two matches.
[L004] RONALDO: Two matches? What am I supposed to do, become ordinary?
[L005] BRUNO: You could sit down.

[Ronaldo starts to sit on the bench, gets within two inches of the seat, then springs back upright. Rubber shoe squeak. Hold one beat on Bruno's deadpan face.]

[L006] RONALDO: That bench started it.
[L007] BRUNO: It's furniture, Cris.
[L008] RONALDO: Furniture with an agenda.

## SCENE 2 — THE WORLD'S CALMEST PRESS CONFERENCE

INT. PORTUGAL PRESS ROOM — DAY.

[Ronaldo has called his own press conference, adds a giant gold number seven to the lectern. Bruno, exhausted, is filming it on his phone. Insert absurd slides: WHY I SHOULD PLAY; a photo of Ronaldo; an even bigger photo of Ronaldo; a crown.]

[L009] RONALDO: I would like to announce I am completely calm.
[L010] BRUNO: You sent the manager seventy-four voice notes.
[L011] RONALDO: One was a song.
[L012] BRUNO: They were all the same song.
[L013] RONALDO: It is a classic.

[Half-second Bruno glance into the lens. Zoom slowly toward Ronaldo as he delivers his master plan.]

[L014] RONALDO: I will prove I am the best in the world.
[L015] BRUNO: You said you already were.
[L016] RONALDO: Exactly. Imagine how good I could be if I improved.

## SCENE 3 — SEVEN THOUSAND REPS

INT. TRAINING GYM — DAY.

[Whip-pan into the gym; Ronaldo has changed into training kit. ACTUALLY animate his dumbbell grip and rise, planted shoes and elbows; counter at REP COUNT: 7. Bruno leans on the wall.]

[L017] RONALDO: Suspension is fuel. Seven thousand reps before lunch!
[L018] BRUNO: You've done seven.
[L019] RONALDO: Seven thousand mentally.
[L020] BRUNO: That's not how numbers work.
[L021] RONALDO: Tell that to my goal tally.

[He does a huge flex. The gold trophy-shaped shaker tumbles off the bench. Clunk. Bruno looks down then up.]

[L022] BRUNO: Are we done now?
[L023] RONALDO: Not until the world agrees!

## SCENE 4 — SELF-SELECTION

INT. AIRPORT DEPARTURES — EVENING.

[Bruno pushes a luggage trolley; Ronaldo walks beside him carrying a giant framed photo of himself. He unrolls a Portugal formation sheet; all eleven player photos are Ronaldo. One insert, not eleven extra actor rigs.]

[L024] BRUNO: Where are you going?
[L025] RONALDO: Portugal. To select the team myself.
[L026] BRUNO: You've put yourself in all eleven positions.
[L027] RONALDO: Yes. Finally, a balanced squad.
[L028] BRUNO: Who's in goal?
[L029] RONALDO: Me. Best keeper in the world.
[L030] BRUNO: And manager?
[L031] RONALDO: Also me. Very good man-management.

[Bruno gets a phone ping: REMOVED FROM PORTUGAL TEAM CHAT. Ronaldo's smile disappears; mouth stays closed through the reaction.]

[L032] BRUNO: They have removed you from the team WhatsApp.
[L033] RONALDO: They can suspend my football. They cannot suspend my greatness.

[Ronaldo's own phone pings: ACCOUNT TEMPORARILY SUSPENDED. Unbroken one-second horrified look, mouth closed.]

[L034] RONALDO: OH, FOR FUCK'S SAKE!

[Smash-cut to black MID-shout; tiny airport announcement ding. END.]
